# Build a licensed, honest Indian signals platform

Kronos is a small, MIT-licensed, GPT-style foundation model that reads OHLCV candles as tokens and forecasts future candles. It is real, cheap to run and legally usable in a commercial product. Every independent out-of-sample test published by October 2026, though, finds **no usable per-stock directional edge**. It calls direction about as well as a coin flip, does worse than "always up", loses to decades-old volatility models and gives poorly calibrated ranges. So the platform should not be "Kronos picks stocks". Build it on three things the evidence supports. The first is a **gradient-boosted cross-sectional ranker** (LightGBM on momentum, quality, liquidity and flow factors), with Kronos, Chronos-2 and HAR-style volatility only as *features*. The second is **rules-based baskets** modelled on documented Indian momentum and index methodology. The third is a **timestamped, after-cost public track record**, the one thing incumbents with unverifiable "85% success" claims lack. The binding constraint is regulation, not technology. Buy/sell calls with stop loss and target, and curated weighted baskets, count as research under SEBI's Research Analyst (RA) rules, free or paid. In practice that means a corporate RA registration through BSE before launch, AI-use disclosure, human sign-off and PaRRVA enrolment for any performance claim. A Node.js + MongoDB + Next.js + Python team can build the whole stack from open-source parts for low lakhs a year in infrastructure. The real fixed costs are licensing (an RA renewal fee of about ₹5 lakh for a body corporate, deposits of ₹1–10 lakh, and NSE data fees from about ₹1.2 lakh per display medium) and the compliance team that comes with them.

## Kronos is a credible model with no demonstrated trading edge

Kronos (github.com/shiyu-coder/Kronos) is a two-stage model from a Tsinghua team. First, a tokenizer uses **Binary Spherical Quantization** to compress each candle's open/high/low/close/volume into a coarse-plus-fine pair of discrete tokens. Second, a decoder-only Transformer predicts the next token autoregressively, which makes it a language model whose "words" are candles ([GitHub](https://github.com/shiyu-coder/Kronos)). It was pre-trained on **more than 12 billion K-line records from 45+ exchanges** across 7 timeframes ([NeurIPS 2025](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2025/132504)). The paper was accepted at AAAI 2026 ([AAAI](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/39730)). Three sizes are open on Hugging Face under the NeoQuasar namespace: **mini (4.1M parameters, 2,048-bar context), small (24.7M) and base (102.3M), both with 512-bar context**. The 499M "large" model is not released. Code is MIT, and users describe the released weights as MIT ([GitHub](https://github.com/shiyu-coder/Kronos); [Issue #273](https://github.com/shiyu-coder/Kronos/issues/273)). Its API takes a price DataFrame with timestamps and returns forecast OHLCV bars. One property is unusual among time-series models: it generates full candles, so sampled paths include intraday highs and lows. That is exactly what a "does the target get hit before the stop" calculation needs. No source lists NSE or BSE in the pre-training corpus ([themenonlab](https://themenonlab.blog/blog/kronos-foundation-model-financial-markets-candlestick)). Indian stocks are therefore effectively out-of-distribution until you fine-tune on them. The 512-bar context covers about two years of daily bars, or under seven sessions of 5-minute NSE bars.

The authors' headline numbers are relative gains: **+93% RankIC over the best foundation-model baseline**, 9% lower volatility error, and wins over 25 baselines ([NeurIPS 2025](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2025/132504)). The absolute levels are tiny. NeurIPS slides put Kronos-large's RankIC at roughly 0.027 against TimeMoE's 0.014, and at 0.068 against Moirai's 0.053 ([NeurIPS slides](https://neurips.cc/media/neurips-2025/Slides/130441.pdf)). A rank correlation of 0.03 can pay for a diversified, low-cost long-short book. It cannot support "buy this stock now" calls to retail users.

The independent record is uniformly negative. A frozen Kronos-small test on 30 US large-caps covered **11,340 out-of-sample forecasts from July 2024 to December 2025**. It found **49.5% daily direction accuracy against 54.6% for simply predicting "up"**. Nominal 90% prediction intervals **covered only 68%** of outcomes. Adding Kronos to a HAR volatility model lifted R² by just 0.013 ([imanly97/kronos-evaluation](https://github.com/imanly97/kronos-evaluation)). A peer-reviewed 2026 study of about 5,000 Chinese A-shares found zero-shot Kronos volatility error **2.4–2.6× worse than HAR-RV**, last every year. Fine-tuning made it *worse*, and a Kronos+GARCH hybrid was statistically indistinguishable from GARCH alone ([Finance Research Letters](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1544612326011347)). A forex/CFD test on 749 forecasts hit 50.2% direction, lost money before costs, and its most confident forecasts were among the worst ([Medium](https://florinelchis.medium.com/testing-kronos-price-prediction-model-on-forex-and-cfds-b9d4d0e76e3c)). The authors' own repo labels its backtest "a demonstration, not a production system" ([GitHub](https://github.com/shiyu-coder/Kronos)).

There are engineering hazards too. Fine-tuning code leaked future data into normalisation. That was fixed in April 2026 only in the Qlib pipeline ([Issue #227](https://github.com/shiyu-coder/Kronos/issues/227)). The CSV fine-tuning path (`finetune_csv/finetune_base_model.py`) still normalises over the forecast window at HEAD. The public `predict` call averages sampled paths, so you must patch it to get a distribution. Inference has no KV cache. The repo has had **no commit since 13 April 2026** despite about 40k stars ([opensourcealternatives.to](https://www.opensourcealternatives.to/item/kronos)). Treat it as a frozen research baseline that you own and maintain.

The candid conclusion is that Kronos earns a place only as an **ensemble feature**: expected-return z-score, probability of an up move, and dispersion across sampled paths as a regime or volatility input. It must be fine-tuned on adjusted NSE data with the leak patched, and evaluated only on data after its roughly June 2024 pre-training cutoff to avoid contamination. It stays in the model only if it adds information out-of-sample after costs. A cost estimate from the notes, not a benchmark, puts a nightly 10-day, 20-path forecast for about 2,000 NSE stocks with Kronos-small at **roughly 10–20 minutes on one T4/3090-class GPU**, and hours on CPU. One commercial user runs Kronos-base on 100 US stocks on a single RTX 3090 ([Issue #273](https://github.com/shiyu-coder/Kronos/issues/273)). An honest NSE walk-forward study of Kronos would also be novel. No Indian broker, fintech or paper has published one, and the only Indian repo reports no accuracy at all ([GitHub](https://github.com/47thtechcorner/RayCodes_Kronos)). Publishing it is cheap credibility.

## Trees beat transformers for returns; foundation models earn their keep on risk

The wider open-source landscape tells the same story. A June 2026 study of TimeGPT, TimesFM-2.5, Moirai-2.0, Chronos and Chronos-2 on US stocks found these models useful as cheap priors but found they **"do not reliably produce statistically significant alpha"** ([arXiv 2606.27100](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.27100)). A large global study found off-the-shelf models perform poorly on daily excess returns. Finance-pretrained versions help, and **tree ensembles beat both linear and neural models** at next-day return prediction ([arXiv 2511.18578](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.18578v1)). Foundation models do better on risk. Fine-tuned TimesFM beat GARCH for daily Value-at-Risk on S&P 100 stocks ([arXiv 2410.11773](https://www.arxiv.org/pdf/2410.11773)). Reinforcement learning is the weakest option. FinRL's own authors name backtest overfitting as a core obstacle ([FinRL-Meta](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2022/file/0bf54b80686d2c4dc0808c2e98d430f7-Paper-Datasets_and_Benchmarks.pdf)). A 2026 study found every RL agent underperformed its benchmark out-of-sample ([arXiv 2603.29086](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.29086v1)). Even Qlib's canonical LightGBM baseline swings from about 0% to +29% excess return depending on universe and period ([AlphaSeek](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.13913); [arXiv 2605.28359](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.28359)). Only after-cost, out-of-sample numbers mean anything.

Licences are the second filter, and the best-known names fail it. **TimesFM 3.0 weights (August 2026) are non-commercial** outside Google Cloud ([google-research/timesfm](https://github.com/google-research/timesfm)). **Moirai 2.0 weights are reportedly CC BY-NC** ([beri.net](https://www.beri.net/article/timesfm-vs-chronos-vs-moirai-time-series-foundation-model-licence-bigquery-sagemaker-cost-2026)). **Chronos-2 is Apache-2.0** and led GIFT-Eval with a 79.8% average win rate ([Chronos-2 paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.15821)). On the library side, **vectorbt carries a Commons Clause** that bars selling a product whose value derives substantially from it ([vectorbt licence](https://vectorbt.dev/terms/license/)). Backtesting.py is AGPL and backtrader is GPL. The original pandas-ta faced archiving unless funded by July 2026 ([pandas-ta.dev](https://www.pandas-ta.dev/)).

| Job | Recommended (licence) | Avoid / why |
|---|---|---|
| Cross-sectional stock ranking (the core signal) | **LightGBM** (MIT); Qlib (MIT) as research harness and source of Alpha158 factor definitions; XGBoost/CatBoost (Apache-2.0) as alternatives | FinRL/RL as a production signal (overfitting, fails out-of-sample) |
| Candle-path features, P(up), path dispersion | **Kronos-small/base** (MIT), fine-tuned on NSE, leak patched | Kronos-large (unreleased); raw Kronos as a standalone signal |
| Volatility / range bands / risk | **HAR-RV and GARCH baselines first**, then **Chronos-2** (Apache-2.0), TimesFM 2.5 (Apache-2.0), IBM Granite TTM (Apache-2.0, CPU-cheap) | TimesFM 3.0 self-hosted (non-commercial), Moirai 2.0 (NC) |
| Announcement/filing summarisation, event tagging | **Qwen 3.5** mid-size (Apache-2.0), **DeepSeek V4/R1** (MIT), **Gemma 4** (Apache-2.0) ([codersera](https://codersera.com/blog/open-source-llms-landscape-2026/); [datanorth](https://datanorth.ai/blog/the-best-open-source-llm-in-2026)) | Llama 4 for redistribution (custom licence, use restrictions) |
| Hindi / Indic explanations | **Sarvam 30B** (Apache-2.0, 22 Indian languages) ([Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/sarvam-105b-Sarvam-30b-everything-you-need-to-know)) | Free API tiers with user data (Gemini free tier may train on prompts) |
| Bulk headline sentiment | Fine-tuned FinBERT / multilingual encoder on your own labelled Indian headlines | One LLM call per headline (cost, non-determinism) |
| "Explain this stock" assistant | TradingAgents as a template ([arXiv 2412.20138](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.20138v4)) | Using it as a signal generator (look-ahead leakage) |
| Basket optimisation | **skfolio** or **Riskfolio-Lib** (BSD-3) on **cvxpy** (Apache-2.0) | — |
| Backtesting (customer-facing) | Custom vectorised Polars/pandas engine, zipline-reloaded (Apache-2.0), NautilusTrader (LGPL) | vectorbt (Commons Clause), Backtesting.py (AGPL), backtrader (GPL) in the paid product |
| Indicators | **TA-Lib** (BSD); pandas-ta-classic fork as fallback | Original pandas-ta (maintenance risk) |

Several of these licences (Chronos, TTM, skfolio, Riskfolio, TA-Lib) were not re-verified at source during research. Check each LICENSE file before shipping. None of the studies above tested Indian equities. Every edge claim has to be re-earned on NSE data.

## SEBI makes a corporate RA licence the price of admission

The legal analysis decides what can be free. Stock-specific buy/sell calls with entry, stop loss and target are research under the SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations. The December 2024 amendment defines an RA as someone providing research "for consideration", and **consideration expressly includes non-cash benefits** ([SCC Online](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2024/12/19/sebi-research-analyst-third-amendment-regulations-2024/)). A free tier that markets a paid tier, earns referral fees or monetises data is plausibly "for consideration". SEBI has treated free Telegram channels as funnels. In April 2026 it barred three operators and ordered **about ₹9.02 crore refunded** ([Moneylife](https://moneylife.in/article/902-crore-refund-40-lakh-penalty-sebi-bars-3-telegram-channel-operators/80354.html)). Earlier it ordered about ₹54 crore disgorged from an "education" academy that gave stock-specific advice ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/markets/news/sebi-impounds-rs-53-crore-illegal-gains-from-asmita-patel-five-others-125020701263_1.html)).

Unregistered "education" is boxed in as well. It may not name securities using price data from the preceding three months ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/markets/news/sebi-finfluencer-circular-live-stock-data-market-education-rules-125013000571_1.html)). Regulated entities (brokers, AMCs, RAs) may not associate with unregistered advice-givers, including through referral deals ([Legal500](https://www.legal500.com/developments/thought-leadership/securities-law-update-sebi-imposes-restrictions-on-intermediaries-and-finfluencers/)). An unregistered startup would therefore be cut off from broker partnerships as well as from making calls.

Baskets fit the same licence. Since January 2025 a **model portfolio, meaning a basket with recommended weights plus a research report, is a research service under the RA regime**. It requires a documented methodology, a review frequency, benchmarking, a factsheet and performance validation ([AZB & Partners](https://www.azbpartners.com/?p=86619); [TeamLease RegTech](https://teamleaseregtech.com/updates/article/38394/sebi-issued-the-guidelines-for-research-analysts-ra)). One corporate RA licence can therefore cover both the signals and non-personalised core/satellite baskets. Investment Adviser registration becomes necessary only when the product personalises, for example by risk-profiling a user and steering them to a basket or tailoring weights. smallcase shows the alternative. It operates as a technology layer while SEBI-registered managers own the portfolios ([smallcase](https://www.smallcase.com/learn/who-are-smallcase-managers)). That route works if you would rather list baskets as a manager than own the customer.

The licensing path runs as follows. Apply through **BSE, the RA Administration and Supervisory Body** ([Moneylife](https://moneylife.in/article/sebi-recognises-bse-limited-for-enhanced-supervision-of-research-analysts-and-investment-advisers/74646.html)). The five-year renewal fee for a body corporate is **₹5,000 to SEBI plus ₹4,95,000 to BSE**, against ₹1,000 + ₹9,000 for individuals ([TeamLease RegTech](https://teamleaseregtech.com/updates/article/58080/bse-notified-regarding-the-renewal-process-for-registration-of-research-analysts-ras/)). Initial fees for a body corporate were not confirmed, so budget the same order. Post a **deposit of ₹1 lakh for up to 150 clients, rising to ₹10 lakh above 1,000** ([TeamLease RegTech](https://teamleaseregtech.com/updates/article/38394/sebi-issued-the-guidelines-for-research-analysts-ra)). Staff NISM-certified analysts and a principal officer. Respect the ₹1.51 lakh per-family annual fee cap, which will not bind at retail prices. Advance billing is limited to one year, only with client consent, with pro-rata refunds ([Treelife](https://treelife.in/news/sebi-relaxes-advance-fee-rules-for-investment-advisers-and-research-analysts-boosting-flexibility/)). Keep research clients segregated from any distribution business. **Disclose the extent of AI use** in the terms and conditions, and remember the RA stays fully responsible for AI output and data security ([Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/markets/investment-advisers-research-analysts-should-disclose-ai-tool-usage-to-clients-sebi)). SEBI's June 2025 AI/ML consultation proposes human oversight, shadow testing, bias controls and model accountability. It was not confirmed as final by October 2026, but you should build to it now ([Taxmann](https://www.taxmann.com/post/blog/sebi-proposes-ai-ml-governance-framework-for-securities-markets)). The model followed by Univest, an AI shortlist approved by registered analysts, is the pattern to copy ([Univest](https://univest.in/blogs/univest-stock-picks-review-how-research-supports-investment-ideas)).

Two newer regimes shape the product. First, **PaRRVA**: CARE Ratings is the verification agency and NSE is its data centre. Regular services began **4 May 2026**, and regulated entities had three months to enrol if they want to keep communicating past performance ([News on AIR](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/sebi-operationalises-parrva-to-boost-transparency-and-investor-protection/)). After about two years, only PaRRVA-verified metrics may be shown ([Kotak Neo](https://www.kotakneo.com/news/market-news/sebi-parrva-verify-returns-curb-mis-selling/)). The "transparent track record" strategy therefore has to flow through PaRRVA, not just a self-published dashboard. Second, the **retail algo framework** of 4 February 2025, fully applicable by about 1 April 2026, makes brokers principals and algo providers their agents. **Black-box algo providers must be RAs** with a research report per algo ([KS&K](https://ksandk.com/corporate/sebis-new-regulatory-framework-for-retail-algorithmic-trading/); [SEBI](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/sep-2025/extension-of-timeline-for-implementation-of-sebi-circular-dated-february-04-2025-on-safer-participation-of-retail-investors-in-algorithmic-trading-_96979.html)). One-click execution where the user confirms each order on the broker's page avoids this. Auto-execution does not. DPDP Rules notified in November 2025 bring core consent, notice and breach duties in around May 2027 ([Hogan Lovells](https://www.hoganlovells.com/en/publications/indias-digital-personal-data-protection-act-2023-brought-into-force-)).

That produces a clear free/paid split. "Free" here means *free of charge but issued by the registered RA*, except for the unregistered tools in the first row.

| Feature | Before RA registration | After RA registration | Free or paid |
|---|---|---|---|
| User-configured screener, indicator charts, backtests on the user's own rules, user-built baskets executed via broker | Likely acceptable (no SEBI safe harbour; confirm with counsel) | Yes | **Free** (acquisition; incumbents give this away) |
| Hindi/English education | Only with price data ≥3 months old | Yes, with live data | **Free** |
| Delayed or closed-trade signal ledger, performance stats | No (return claims) | Yes, via PaRRVA | **Free** (trust engine) |
| Live swing/positional calls with entry, SL, targets, exit alerts | No | Yes, with AI disclosure + human sign-off | **Paid** |
| Core/satellite model-portfolio baskets with weights, rebalance alerts | No | Yes (model-portfolio framework) | Core basket **free**, satellite/thematic **paid** |
| Personalised allocation by risk profile | No | No — needs **IA** registration | Paid, later phase |
| Auto-execution of platform signals | No | Needs RA + exchange empanelment via brokers | Paid, later phase |

Pricing benchmarks: Univest sells RA calls at **about ₹6/day** with ₹1 trials ([Univest](https://univest.in/blogs/univest-advisory-review-how-the-app-delivers-stock-market-ideas)). smallcase managers typically charge ₹100–500 a month ([equitiesindia](https://equitiesindia.com/learn/wealth-products/smallcase-explained)). Screener Premium is about ₹4,999 a year ([Strike Money](https://www.strike.money/reviews/screener-in)). Zerodha now gives Streak free to its users ([TradingQnA](https://tradingqna.com/t/streak-unlimited-is-now-accessible-for-all-zerodha-users/159215?page=2)). Screening has been commoditised; actionable calls and managed baskets are what people pay for. A ₹199–499 monthly plan, with no multi-year lock-ins, fits that market.

## Free data powers research; display and redistribution must be licensed

Data has its own licensing layer, separate from SEBI. NSE's website terms **ban automated scraping and redistribution without written permission** ([NSE Terms](https://www.nseindia.com/static/nse-terms-of-use)). yfinance describes Yahoo's API as **personal use only** ([yfinance README](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/ranaroussi/yfinance@main/README.md)). Bhavcopy wrappers like jugaad-data, nselib and nsefin are fine for internal research. jugaad-data supports the new site and caches requests ([PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/jugaad-data)). Note that bhavcopy switched to the UDiFF format in July 2024 ([eod2](https://github.com/BennyThadikaran/eod2/blob/main/README.md)). For a commercial product, the clean MVP sources are broker APIs used within their terms. **Kite Connect costs ₹500/month** with historical candles and streaming ([Zerodha](https://support.zerodha.com/category/trading-and-markets/kite-api/articles/what-are-the-charges-for-kite-apis)). **Upstox WebSocket data is free** to account holders ([Upstox](https://community.upstox.com/t/i-just-wanted-to-clarify-websocket-is-free-of-cost-or-do-i-need-to-pay-for-it-for-real-time-data-if-any-charges-for-this-then-where-i-can-get-that-information-please-respond/8213)). Dhan's data API costs ₹499/month ([Dhan](https://dhan.co/support/platforms/dhanhq-api/how-does-the-dhanhq-data-api-subscription-work/)). All of these license data to the account holder. Fanning one feed out to every user is redistribution.

Displaying even **15-minute delayed NSE data costs about ₹1,20,000 per segment per display medium**, with website and app charged separately ([NSE tariff](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com//web/mediaattachment/2026-04/Download_1_min_Snapshot_15_minute_delayed_Tariff.pdf)). NSE also has a separate **Non-Display policy** covering data used in algorithms ([NSE](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/web/sites/default/files/inline-files/Non_Display_Policy.pdf)). Ask NSE Data directly whether EOD-derived signals need a licence. Authorised vendors such as TrueData license data for internal use, with redistribution negotiated separately ([TrueData](https://truedata.in/market-data-apis)). The cheapest compliant MVP has four parts: generate signals on EOD bars, show EOD prices or "live price in your broker" deep links, let users who connect their own broker see live data under their own entitlement, and buy a delayed-display licence once traction justifies about ₹2.4 lakh for web plus app.

The other inputs are mostly free primary disclosures. **NSE RSS feeds** cover announcements, results, board meetings and corporate actions. The corporate-actions feed is what you need to back-adjust bhavcopy for splits and bonuses ([NSE RSS](https://www.nseindia.com/rss-feed)). Listed companies file **XBRL** results, the legally clean source for fundamentals ([NSE](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/web/sites/default/files/inline-files/Circular%20draft%20XBRL%20release%2031032023.pdf)). Screener.in by contrast is licensed for personal, non-commercial viewing only ([Screener ToS](https://www.screener.in/guides/terms)). AMCs publish monthly portfolios within about 10 days of month-end via AMFI ([AMFI](https://www.amfiindia.com/online-center/download-factsheets)). For execution, **Kite's offsite basket** sends users to Zerodha's exchange-approved order page at no cost, capped at 10 stocks per Publisher basket ([Kite docs](https://kite.trade/docs/connect/v3/basket/); [Kite Publisher](https://kite.trade/docs/connect/v3/publisher)). **smallcase Gateway** reaches about 20 brokers covering about 97% of active accounts, on negotiated licence terms ([smallcase](https://www.smallcase.com/blog/supercharging-portfolio-insights-and-trading-for-liquide/)).

## Rules-first signals and index-style baskets, with Kronos on probation

### Signals: rank, gate, size, and let costs decide

The signal engine should target **end-of-day swing and positional trades in liquid equities** (Nifty 200/500 or F&O-eligible names), not intraday or options. The evidence favours momentum. Indian momentum profits persist for up to six months and are not explained by CAPM or Fama-French ([Sehgal & Jain 2015](https://ejournal.usm.my/aamjaf/article/view/aamjaf_vol11-no1-2015_3)), and part of it is sector momentum ([Sehgal & Jain 2011](https://www.emerald.com/insight/content/doi/10.1108/09727981111129327/full/html)). Avoiding options is also a positioning choice. **More than 91% of individual F&O traders lost money in FY25, about ₹1.06 lakh crore in total** ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/markets/news/net-losses-of-traders-in-fo-widens-in-fy25-sebi-study-125070701221_1.html)).

The engine has four layers:

1. **Ranking.** Each night, a LightGBM ranker scores the universe on Alpha158-style price factors, sector-relative momentum, delivery percentage, FII/DII flows, XBRL quality and value metrics, and announcement event tags from the LLM. Kronos-derived features (P(up), expected-return z-score, path dispersion) and Chronos-2 or HAR volatility forecasts are candidate columns.
2. **Regime gate.** Nifty's position relative to its 200-day average, plus breadth, sets how many signals are issued. Trend filters mainly reduce drawdowns rather than add return ([Early Retirement Now](https://earlyretirementnow.com/2018/04/25/market-timing-and-risk-management-part-2-momentum)), which is the right reason to use one.
3. **Stops and targets.** Stops sit at 1.5–3× ATR or below the swing low. Targets are set in R-multiples, with a trailing stop and a time stop. Present these as risk conventions, not alpha. No rigorous study of ATR stops in India exists, and frequent stop rules have been shown to add little value to trend strategies ([CXO Advisory](https://www.cxoadvisory.com/technical-trading/higher-measurement-frequency-and-stop-losses-for-trend-followers)).
4. **Path-based probability.** Kronos's sampled OHLC paths can estimate **P(target hit before stop)** for each candidate. That number is shown only after conformal or isotonic recalibration, because raw intervals cover 68% where they claim 90%.

Every call goes to a registered analyst for sign-off before it is published, pre-open.

Model governance is what separates this from a tips app. Use **purged walk-forward validation**. Compare against fixed baselines: always-up, Nifty buy-and-hold, a plain Nifty200 Momentum 30 replica, and LightGBM without the foundation-model features. Charge **Indian costs explicitly**: brokerage, STT, stamp duty, exchange fees, GST and slippage. Use survivorship-free universes and corporate-action-adjusted prices. A feature family, Kronos included, enters production only if it raises out-of-sample IC and after-cost returns on post-mid-2024 NSE data, and it is removed if live IC decays. Write that inclusion rule into the AI disclosure. It is honest, and it matches SEBI's proposed testing and oversight expectations.

### Baskets: copy the index rulebook, then improve it slowly

For baskets, NSE has already published a replicable satellite template. **Nifty200 Momentum 30** takes the top 30 stocks by a volatility-adjusted blend of 50% 6-month and 50% 12-month returns, weights them by free-float cap × score (capped at the lower of 5% or 5× the stock's cap weight), and rebalances semi-annually in June and December ([NSE factsheet](https://www.niftyindices.com/Factsheet/Factsheet_Nifty200_Momentum30.pdf)). NSE's 2026 whitepaper finds quarterly rebalancing adds **20–50% more annual turnover** ([NSE whitepaper](https://www.niftyindices.com/docs/default-source/indices/nifty200-momentum-30/momentum-strategy-whitepaper_2026.pdf)).

A sensible lineup has three tiers:

- **Free long-term core basket (60–80% of a user's allocation).** A Nifty 50/100 ETF, or a large-cap quality/low-volatility basket, rebalanced annually or on more than 5 percentage points of drift.
- **Paid momentum satellite.** A Momentum-30-style basket with your own refinements.
- **Paid thematic satellites.** For example manufacturing, defence or PSU banks, with a regime overlay that cuts satellite exposure below the 200-day average.

Build the baskets in skfolio or Riskfolio-Lib, with cvxpy constraints for sector caps, minimum ticket size and stock count. Each basket needs the model-portfolio factsheet, a benchmark and a documented methodology. Keep rebalancing semi-annual by default. Short-term capital-gains tax (reported at 20% for holdings under a year, a rate to verify) and costs punish churn in retail accounts. Using "Nifty" names in basket branding may need an NSE Indices licence, so describe these baskets as momentum-style rather than as index replicas.

## A Python research core behind a Node.js product shell

The split follows the team's existing skills. **Python owns everything quantitative** and runs as batch jobs. **Node.js owns users, money, entitlements and delivery.** **MongoDB is the shared system of record.** **Next.js is the product.** The two halves communicate through MongoDB collections and a job queue, never through synchronous calls on the request path. Signal generation is an EOD batch, so the user-facing API never waits on a model.

| Layer | Component | Specific choices |
|---|---|---|
| Ingestion (Python) | EOD bars, corporate actions, F&O, delivery, bulk/block deals, FII/DII | jugaad-data / nselib / nsefin for internal research; Kite Connect (₹500/mo) or Upstox APIs for licensed pulls; NSE RSS (feedparser) for announcements and corporate actions; XBRL parser (e.g. Arelle) for results; AMFI files for MF holdings |
| Storage | Bars, features, signals, users | **MongoDB time-series collections** for bars and features; Parquet on object storage (read with Polars/DuckDB) for training sets; MongoDB documents for signals, baskets, users, subscriptions |
| Features (Python) | Factors, indicators, adjustments | Polars/pandas, **TA-Lib**, Qlib Alpha158 definitions, an in-house corporate-action adjuster |
| Models (Python) | Ranking, volatility, paths | **LightGBM** ranker; **Kronos-small** (PyTorch, fine-tuned, patched normalisation, `sample_paths` patch); **Chronos-2** / Granite TTM; HAR-RV and GARCH baselines (statsmodels / arch); MAPIE-style conformal calibration |
| Text (Python) | Announcements, sentiment, Hindi | **Qwen 3.5** or **DeepSeek** via vLLM (Ollama in dev); **Sarvam 30B** for Hindi summaries; fine-tuned FinBERT/multilingual encoder for headline sentiment |
| Portfolio (Python) | Basket construction | **skfolio** / **Riskfolio-Lib** + **cvxpy** |
| Backtest & evaluation (Python) | Walk-forward, costs, reports | Custom vectorised Polars engine for anything customer-facing (vectorbt allowed internally only); MLflow for experiment and model registry, which doubles as the SEBI AI/ML model inventory |
| Orchestration | Nightly DAG after the 15:30 IST close | Prefect or cron-driven Python workers; BullMQ (Redis) for Node-side jobs |
| Compliance workflow (Node) | Analyst review queue, approvals, audit log | Express or NestJS service; **hash-chained, append-only signal ledger** in MongoDB with a daily root hash published publicly; export feed for PaRRVA |
| Product API (Node) | Auth, entitlements, billing | Zod-validated REST; Razorpay subscriptions with refund flows matching SEBI advance-fee rules |
| Delivery | Alerts | Web push (PWA), email, in-app; WhatsApp click-to-chat links (avoid Telegram-channel optics given enforcement history) |
| Frontend (Next.js) | Web + PWA, Hindi/English | Next.js App Router, next-intl (en/hi), TanStack Query, lightweight-charts for candles; Kite offsite basket / smallcase Gateway for execution |

Three design choices matter more than the library list. First, the **ledger is the product**. Each call is written with entry, stop, targets and analyst ID before the market opens, chained by hash, and never edited. Exits and P&L are computed mechanically, net of costs. Start the private ledger on day one of research, months before registration, so that a long out-of-sample record exists when PaRRVA-verified disclosure becomes possible. Second, **inference is offline and cheap**. LightGBM, TTM and Chronos-2 small run on CPU, and Kronos needs one GPU for under an hour a night, which can be a spot or rented instance. Third, **user data never touches free LLM tiers**. Gemini's free tier may train on prompts ([OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/blog/tutorials/free-llm-apis-compared/)), and free tiers cap out at 50–1,500 requests a day, far below the daily flow of NSE/BSE announcements. Self-host or pay per token.

## Roadmap: register while you research, launch with a record

The sequence is dictated by the licence. Nothing that names a stock goes to the public until the corporate RA is live, so the registration months are used to build the data pipeline and accumulate a private out-of-sample record. Costs below separate **sourced** regulatory and data figures from **rough estimates** (marked "est."). The estimates are planning numbers, not quotes.

Team cost dominates beyond that. The compute bill for the open-source stack is small. Willingness to pay is proven: Univest claims 150k+ paying users, and smallcase earned about **₹106 crore in FY25** ([Entrackr](https://entrackr.com/exclusive/exclusive-smallcase-crosses-rs-100-cr-revenue-mark-in-fy25-9493192)). Uttar Pradesh has about 3 crore trading accounts, second only to Maharashtra ([Angel One](https://oga-prod.angelone.in/news/market-updates/nse-crosses-26-crore-trading-accounts-investor-base-expands-rapidly)), which supports a Hindi-first, tier-2/3 wedge.

## The risks: no edge, leakage, and regulatory drift

The largest risk is that **no model has an edge on NSE after costs**. Nothing in the evidence guarantees that LightGBM factors or Kronos features survive Indian transaction costs out-of-sample. Mitigate by making the baskets' value proposition rules-based diversification rather than alpha, and by publishing results either way. A losing-but-honest ledger is a product. A hidden one is a liability.

The second risk is **leakage and contamination**: the unpatched Kronos CSV pipeline, a pre-training cutoff around mid-2024 that inflates earlier backtests, survivorship bias in current index constituents, and unadjusted bhavcopy creating fake candles around splits.

The third is **calibration and confidence**. Kronos's confident calls were among its worst in one test, so never show raw model confidence to users.

The fourth is **regulatory drift**. The final AI/ML guidelines, PaRRVA metric rules, model-portfolio validation details and the exact free-tool boundary were not read from primary text in this research. The February 2026 RA master circular, GST treatment, SCORES/ODR duties and fee-collection channels (for example BSE's centralised mechanism) must be confirmed with counsel before launch.

The fifth is **data licensing**. Scraped NSE data shown to users breaches site terms, and the Non-Display policy could reach signal generation itself.

The sixth is **model and vendor decay**. Kronos is unmaintained since April 2026, free LLM tiers change monthly, and pandas-ta-style dependencies can vanish. Pin weights and versions, mirror them, and keep classical baselines as the fallback.

The last is **distribution**. Brokers bundle tools for free, and Meta reportedly requires SEBI verification for investment ads ([5paisa](https://www.5paisa.com/news/sebi-action-unregistered-finfluencers)). Paid acquisition therefore also waits on registration.

## Conclusion

The useful reframing is that in Indian retail markets **the moat is verifiability, not prediction**. Open-source models have made forecasting machinery nearly free. Kronos, Chronos-2, LightGBM and Qwen together cost less to run than one analyst's salary. At the same time SEBI has made *unverified* claims nearly impossible to market through PaRRVA, finfluencer and association rules. A startup that treats Kronos as a hypothesis to test rather than an engine to sell can publish the first honest NSE evaluation of financial foundation models. It can also keep a hash-chained, after-cost ledger from day one and launch with a verified record. That turns the evidence against Kronos into a marketing asset and makes regulatory compliance the barrier to entry.

The second implication concerns sequencing. Because the RA licence gates every revenue-bearing feature, registration is the critical path and the months spent waiting are the research window. Founders should file with BSE in the first month, run the walk-forward study during the wait, and set in advance the threshold any model must clear to reach users, with its removal written into the AI disclosure. If no model clears it, the fallback is still a viable business: index-methodology baskets, risk-first swing rules and an honest track record.
